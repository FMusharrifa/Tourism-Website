SEMBUWATHTHA


Sembuwaththa Lake is a hidden gem nestled in the hill country of Sri Lanka, near Matale. This man-made lake, surrounded by lush green tea plantations and dense pine forests, offers a tranquil escape and breathtaking views. Located at an altitude of about 1,140 meters above sea level, Sembuwaththa has become a popular destination for nature lovers and adventurers who seek a peaceful and scenic getaway.
