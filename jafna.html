JAFFNA


Jaffna is a culturally rich and historically significant city in the northernmost part of Sri Lanka. Known as the cultural capital of the Tamil people in Sri Lanka, Jaffna has a unique identity shaped by centuries of tradition, Hindu and Christian influences, colonial history, and resilient communities.
