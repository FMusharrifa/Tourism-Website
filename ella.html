ELLA


Ella is a charming mountain town nestled in Sri Lanka's central highlands, known for its lush greenery, cool climate, and breathtaking scenery. Surrounded by rolling hills, tea plantations, waterfalls, and viewpoints, Ella is a paradise for nature lovers, hikers, and travelers seeking a peaceful escape.
