COCOUNUT TREE HILL,MIRISSA


Coconut Tree Hill in Mirissa is one of the most popular and scenic spots along Sri Lanka’s southern coast. Known for its iconic rows of swaying coconut trees set against the turquoise waters of the Indian Ocean, it’s a favorite among photographers, beachgoers, and anyone looking for a postcard-perfect view of the coast. Located just a short walk from Mirissa Beach, Coconut Tree Hill is accessible yet feels wonderfully remote, making it an ideal spot for relaxation and photography.
