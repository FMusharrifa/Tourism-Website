NUWARA ELIYA


Nuwara Eliya, often called "Little England," is a charming hill station in Sri Lanka's Central Province, known for its cool climate, colonial architecture, and stunning landscapes. Perched at an elevation of about 1,868 meters (6,128 feet), this picturesque town is surrounded by lush tea plantations, misty mountains, and waterfalls, making it one of Sri Lanka's most popular destinations.
