TANGALLE


Tangalle is a serene coastal town in southern Sri Lanka, known for its stunning beaches, relaxed vibe, and natural beauty. It’s a great destination for travelers looking to unwind and enjoy the tropical seaside, along with exploring some unique cultural and natural attractions. Tangalle offers a less crowded alternative to other beach destinations in Sri Lanka, with pristine stretches of sand, turquoise waters, and lush surroundings.
