SIGIRIYA


Sigiriya, or Lion Rock, is a UNESCO World Heritage site in Sri Lanka, known for its ancient rock fortress, stunning frescoes, and impressive ruins. Climbing to the summit offers breathtaking views and a glimpse of the remnants of a royal palace. It's a significant cultural landmark showcasing ancient Sri Lankan architecture.
