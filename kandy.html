KANDY


Kandy, located in the Central Province of Sri Lanka, is a vibrant city rich in history, culture, and natural beauty. Known as the cultural capital of Sri Lanka, Kandy is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the country’s most important religious and cultural centers.
