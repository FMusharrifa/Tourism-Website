WIPATHU NATIONAL PARK


Wilpattu National Park, located in the northwest of Sri Lanka, is one of the oldest and largest national parks in the country, covering approximately 1,317 square kilometers. Known for its unique landscape of natural lakes (or "willus") from which it derives its name, the park is a prime destination for wildlife enthusiasts and nature lovers.
